# DCS Syria Map — Airbase Inventory & Geographic Clusters

## Map Overview

- **Size:** ~1,000 x 980 km
- **Orientation:** NW-to-SE, strong east-west stretch
- **Latitude:** ~31°N (southern Israel/Negev) to 37°N (southern Turkey)
- **Longitude:** ~33°E (Cyprus) to 40°E (western Iraq H3 complex)
- **Countries:** Syria, Lebanon, Cyprus, Turkey (southern strip), Israel, Jordan, Iraq (far east edge)
- **Total airbases:** ~76–80 named facilities (post-2026 update)
- **FARPs:** None pre-placed on the map; all must be placed in the Mission Editor

## FARPs

- **No pre-placed FARPs** ship with the Syria map
- All FARPs must be manually placed as "Invisible FARP" static objects in the Mission Editor
- Once placed, they appear in `world.getAirbases()` as `Airbase.Category.HELIPAD`
- Helipads that exist as terrain objects (usable as landing spots but no logistics without FARP objects):
  - Marj as Sultan North & South, Qabr as Sitt (Damascus area)
  - Naqoura (southern Lebanon)
  - Kharab Ishk (northern Syria)
  - Taftanaz, Minakh
